\### One Star



\*\*Mesu\*\*  

a wen nol na sos.  

mi i mo na nul.  

si i mo win mi.



\*\*IPA\*\*  

/a wen nol na sos/  

/mi i mo na nul/  

/si i mo win mi/



\*\*Literal\*\*  

There exists one small-light in up-stuff.  

I move in not-bright.  

It moves with me.



\*\*English\*\*  

One star in the sky.  

I walk in darkness.  

It walks with me.

\### Two Waters



\*\*Mesu\*\*  

nuw i mo la nus.  

was-lip i mo la nus.  

nus i ku a pan was.



\*\*IPA\*\*  

/nuw i mo la nus/  

/was.lip i mo la nus/  

/nus i ku a pan was/



\*\*Literal\*\*  

Down-water moves toward down-stuff.  

Water-eye moves toward down-stuff.  

Down-stuff receives all water.



\*\*English\*\*  

Rain falls to the ground.  

Tears fall to the ground.  

The ground receives all water.

\### At Dusk



\*\*Mesu\*\*  

lo-me-na e lo na nul we ne.  

mi i se a wen me-to la tu.  

mi i no te a tu.  

lo-me-na e lo.



\*\*IPA\*\*  

/lo.me.na e lo na nul we ne/  

/mi i se a wen me.to la tu/  

/mi i no te a tu/  

/lo.me.na e lo/



\*\*Literal\*\*  

Bright-say-place is bright in dark-of-now.  

I send one say-thing toward you.  

I do not hear you.  

Bright-say-place is bright.



\*\*English\*\*  

At dusk, the screen glows.  

I send one word to you.  

I cannot hear you.  

The screen glows.





\[lo-me-na borrowed for “screen” from your previous poem.]

\### Unspoken



\*\*Mesu\*\*  

tu mep e ti.  

mi mep e ti.  

le-mep a wi-no-me.



\*\*IPA\*\*  

/tu mep e ti/  

/mi mep e ti/  

/le.mep a wi.no.me/



\*\*Literal\*\*  

Your say-part is near.  

My say-part is near.  

Two-mouth is feel-not-say.



\*\*English\*\*  

Your mouth is close.  

My mouth is close.  

A kiss is love without speech.

\### The Speech



\*\*Mesu\*\*  

wo-mep i me a mu me-to.  

nu-lum i se a was la tu.  

tu i wan a was.



\*\*IPA\*\*  

/wo.mep i me a mu me.to/  

/nu.lum i se a was la tu/  

/tu i wan a was/



\*\*Literal\*\*  

Big-mouth speaks many say-things.  

New-person gives water toward you.  

You drink water.



\*\*English\*\*  

The big mouth speaks many words.  

A child brings you water.  

You drink the water.







Wo-mep can be heard as “leader” or “windbag”; the poem leaves that open.

\### Near



\*\*Mesu\*\*  

tu pa i me a wen me-to.  

mi i ki a ni me-to.  

tu e nit.  

ni me-to e ti.



\*\*IPA\*\*  

/tu pa i me a wen me.to/  

/mi i ki a ni me.to/  

/tu e nit/  

/ni me.to e ti/



\*\*Literal\*\*  

You before spoke one say-thing.  

I have this say-thing.  

You are far.  

This say-thing is near.



\*\*English\*\*  

You once said a word.  

I have that word.  

You are far away.  

The word is near.

\### One Foot



\*\*Mesu\*\*  

mi sop e nul.  

mi mop i mo.  

mi i mo.



\*\*IPA\*\*  

/mi sop e nul/  

/mi mop i mo/  

/mi i mo/



\*\*Literal\*\*  

My head is dark.  

My move-part moves.  

I move.



\*\*English\*\*  

My head is dark.  

My foot moves.  

I move.

\### Say-Stuff



\*\*Mesu\*\*  

mi i me a wen me-to.  

ni me-to i mo la tu.  

tu i me a ni me-to la mi.  

mi kan tu a mesu.



\*\*IPA\*\*  

/mi i me a wen me.to/  

/ni me.to i mo la tu/  

/tu i me a ni me.to la mi/  

/mi kan tu a me.su/



\*\*Literal\*\*  

I say one say-thing.  

This say-thing moves toward you.  

You say this say-thing toward me.  

I and you are say-stuff.



\*\*English\*\*  

I say a single word.  

It travels to you.  

You speak it back to me.  

You and I are language.
